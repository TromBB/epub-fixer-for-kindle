EPUB Fixer for Kindle

Repairs the problems that make Send to Kindle drop a book’s table of contents, then checks the result against your original to prove the text and images haven’t changed.

Fixes to apply

Always fixed: odd characters in file names, links that don’t exactly match their files, broken or mis-numbered contents entries, and a missing table of contents.

Books with DRM can’t be changed, and this tool won’t try. A book that’s already in good shape is left exactly as it is.

The checks are this tool’s own, not Amazon’s converter or the full EPUBCheck validator. They compare every chapter’s text character for character, every image byte for byte, the reading order, the contents entries and the metadata.
